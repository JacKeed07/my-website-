Our Focus Areas

Climate & Energy

Working toward net-zero operations by 2040 and 100% renewable electricity in manufacturing.

Circularity & Waste

Designing for reuse and recycling, and working toward zero total waste to landfill.

Timeline Highlights

Intel launched RISE (Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling) with 2030 goals for climate, water, waste, and inclusion.

100% renewable electricity globally, net positive water, and zero waste to landfill across manufacturing.

Intel's commitment to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across global operations.
