Sustainability Timeline

Hover or tap a card to reveal details. On large screens the timeline flows horizontally; on mobile it stacks vertically.

2020

RISE Strategy Launched

Intel introduces its RISE strategy — Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling — with ambitious 2030 goals for climate, water, waste, diversity, and digital readiness.

2022

Net-Zero Commitment

Intel commits to net-zero emissions across global operations by 2040 and invests in renewable energy, energy-efficient manufacturing, and lower-carbon supply chains.

2023

Water Restoration Milestones

Through conservation and watershed partnerships, Intel advances toward net positive water, restoring water in local communities near its sites.

2024

More Efficient Products

New client and data-center processors deliver more performance per watt, helping customers reduce energy use and emissions from computing.

2025

Circularity & Waste

Intel expands circularity — reusing and recycling materials, designing for repair and recovery, and sending zero total manufacturing waste to landfill at key sites.

2030

RISE 2030 Goals

Target year for Intel’s 2030 goals: 100% renewable electricity globally, net positive water, zero waste to landfill, and expanded digital readiness for communities worldwide.

2040

Net-Zero Operations

Intel’s long-term target: net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across global operations, continuing to enable more sustainable technology for the industry.

Why This Matters

Technology shapes how we live, work, and solve global challenges. Intel’s sustainability work shows how a large company can pair innovation with responsibility — cutting emissions, saving water, and designing products that help customers do the same.
